Optimising a Composite Wing Spar

Independent coursework — SolidWorks FEA & design of experiments

Ran a structural FEA and a four-variable Taguchi L16 design-of-experiments study on a CFRP sailplane wing spar, cutting material out of a heavily over-engineered baseline while keeping a safe factor of safety — with a mesh-dependency check and a full manufacturing drawing.

Cessna 210 Centurion — Cargo Redesign

Team project, with an individually authored structural report

Re-sized a light utility aircraft to carry 15–20% more payload after stripping four passenger seats — MTOW, wing loading, wing and tail design. My own contribution goes further: a structural report verifying the vertical tail against CS-23 loads with a V-n diagram, vortex-lattice aerodynamics and a hand-worked stress analysis.

Airbus A350-1000 XWB — Landing Gear

Independent study, under FAR 23/25

Reverse-engineered the main landing gear of the A350-1000 from public aircraft data: touchdown speed, static load distribution, tyre selection from a manufacturer's catalogue, and shock-absorber sizing from the landing energy balance — each step verified by hand.

Rocket-Launched Payload Glider

Team project — engineering design and commercial case

Designed, sized and built a small glider that launches on a model rocket motor and carries a 70 g payload back down intact — six concepts narrowed by a weighted decision matrix, then sized with first-principles aerodynamics. My team and I also built the commercial case for putting it into production: market sizing, unit economics and a funding pitch.

Self-directed

A basic external-aero CFD study on a front wing or diffuser profile.
